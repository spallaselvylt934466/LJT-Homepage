I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML). I am also a Research Intern at MINIMAX.

I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.

## Research Interests & Skills

- Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML)
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 - Present. HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 - 2024. Graduated in June 2024.
